### Kiddy Testing:
- Informal random testing.
- Challenges: Unpredictable, inefficient, not comprehensive.

### Structured Testing Approach:
- **Test Design and Planning**.
- **Test Cases** and Coverage.
- **Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM)**.
